# My Favorite Series: Solo Leveling

## Introduction
My favorite series is **Solo Leveling**. It is an action and fantasy series about a world where hunters fight monsters inside dangerous dungeons.

## About the Story
The story follows **Sung Jin-Woo**, who starts as the weakest hunter in the world. After a dangerous incident in a dungeon, he gains a special ability that allows him to become stronger by leveling up.

## Why I Like It
I like Solo Leveling because of its:
- Amazing fight scenes and my very first manhwa Read before I started reading other series finding the same adrenaline
- Rollercoaster storyline
- Powerful and in depth characters
- Great animation in anime and visuals and art in the manhwa

## My Favorite Character
**Sung Jin-Woo** is my favorite character because of his determination to become stronger and protect the people he cares about.
